🎓 YouTube Topics & Concentration (Economics Lab)

This interactive lab fetches free public metadata from YouTube’s RSS feeds (no API key or login), labels each video into one of 10 economic topics, and measures topic concentration using the Herfindahl–Hirschman Index (HHI).

Step 1 – Choose a YouTube feed

Enter a channel ID, user, or playlist ID. Examples:

  • UC_x5XG1OV2P6uZZ5FSM9Ttw → Google Developers
  • UCLXo7UDZvByw2ixzpQCufnA → The Economist
